# Community Guide

Welcome to the Stria Icons community! We are a group of designers, developers, and creators who collaborate to make Stria Icons one of the most consistent and accessible open-source icon sets.

## Get Involved with Stria Icons

There are several ways you can participate in our community:

*   **Request New Icon Designs**: Need a specific icon for your app? Open a request on GitHub describing the icon concept. Let us know which styles (solid, regular, light, thin, or duotone) you need most.
*   **Share Your Integrations**: Show us how you are using `stria-icons` in your projects! We love seeing our packages (`stria-icons-react`, `stria-icons-blade`, etc.) in production.
*   **Give Design Feedback**: Stria Icons prides itself on strict grid consistency. If you notice any paths, strokes, or sizes that feel out of alignment, let us know so we can refine the raw SVGs.
*   **Discuss Wrapper Development**: Share ideas for improving the build pipelines, compiler performance, or framework integrations.

## Where to Find Us

*   **Discord Server**: Our central hub for real-time design reviews, support, and casual chats.
*   **GitHub Discussions**: The best place for long-form suggestions, feature requests, and community announcements.
*   **Follow on X (Twitter)**: Stay up to date with new release tags, icon expansion updates, and styling announcements.
