# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in the Stria Icons project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our designers, developers, and users include:

*   **Constructive Feedback**: Giving respectful design critiques on SVGs (strokes, alignments, geometry) and wrapper implementations.
*   **Empathy and Kindness**: Being welcoming and supportive of new contributors proposing their first icons or pull requests.
*   **Inclusivity**: Respecting differing viewpoints, architectural preferences, and design approaches.
*   **Focus on Collaboration**: Focusing on what is best for the Stria Icons library and its consistency.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
*   Harassment, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments regarding icon design requests or wrapper code submissions.
*   Public or private harassment of any kind.
*   Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or open-source setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all online and offline spaces associated with the Stria Icons repository, Discord channel, and GitHub discussion pages.
